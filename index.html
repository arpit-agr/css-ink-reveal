Landscape

Artist:
Henri-Edmond Cross
Date:
ca. 1904
Medium:
Watercolor & crayon

A member of the neo-Impressionist circle around Seurat and Signac, Cross adopted their style of individual strokes of pigment, or "tâches." Here, he conveyed a sense of sparkling sunlight by letting areas of the paper shine through between touches of watercolor.
